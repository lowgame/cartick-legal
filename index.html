Good to know

Before you download.

One car is free. Optional CarTick Pro unlocks unlimited cars, comparison and unlimited evidence.

Can CarTick replace a mechanic or PPI?

No. CarTick is a screening and documentation tool. It does not certify a vehicle or replace an independent mechanic, vehicle-history report, title/lien check or professional pre-purchase inspection.

Where are my inspection photos stored?

Your vehicles, answers, notes and inspection photos stay on your device unless you choose to share a brief.

Can I start for free?

Yes. One car is free. Additional cars, comparison and unlimited evidence require optional Pro purchases.
