CarTick Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-26

CarTick is a personal used-car inspection checklist. Vehicle records, checklist answers, notes, and inspection photos are stored locally on your device. CarTick does not require an account and does not upload your inspection content.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not upload vehicles, VIN, licence plates, notes, or photos to a cloud database operated by us.
  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs in the app.

Data processed on device

  • Vehicles, checklist progress, notes, photos, and preferences stay in the app’s local storage.
  • Optional exports and backups you create remain under your control on device or in files you share.

Purchases

Purchase validation may be processed by Apple and our subscription infrastructure (RevenueCat). Your vehicle inspection content is not included in purchase validation.
